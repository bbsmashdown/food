## Ingredients

- 2 | cup | rolled oats | dry
- 2 | | carrots | produce
- 3 | | Medjool dates | produce
- 1 | tsp | cinnamon | dry
- 1/8 | tsp | nutmeg | dry
- 1 1/2 | tbsp | ground flaxseed | dry
- 1 | tbsp | chia seeds | dry
- 2 1/4 | cup | unsweetened almond milk | dairy
- 3 | tbsp | maple syrup | dry
- 1 | tsp | vanilla extract | dry

## Steps

1. Peel and finely grate the carrots (you want about 1 cup). Pit and finely chop the dates.
2. Mix the oats, carrots, dates, cinnamon, nutmeg, flaxseed and chia seeds together in a large mixing bowl.
3. Add the almond milk, maple syrup and vanilla and mix to combine thoroughly.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Uncover and serve.
5. Optional: for a more traditional carrot cake flavor, or if you can't find dates, swap the dates for 1/2 cup raisins.
6. Optional: for babies, briefly cook on the stove to soften the raw carrots and oats, or microwave for 30 to 90 seconds, checking that the heat level is safe. It's good warm for adults, too.
