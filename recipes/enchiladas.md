## Ingredients

- 2 | can | black beans | dry
- 2 | can | red enchilada sauce | dry
- 10 | | flour tortillas | bread
- 3 | cup | shredded Monterey Jack | dairy
- 1 | | red bell pepper | produce
- 1 | | white onion | produce
- 1 | cup | sour cream | dairy
- 2 | tsp | chili powder | dry
- 1 | tsp | cumin | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Cook the onion and pepper, then stir in the beans, spices and a cup of sauce.
3. Fill and roll the tortillas into a baking dish, top with remaining sauce and cheese.
4. Bake 25 minutes. Serve with sour cream.
