## Ingredients

- 2 | lb | chicken thighs | meat
- 1 | cup | plain yogurt | dairy
- 1 | can | crushed tomatoes | dry
- 0.5 | cup | heavy cream | dairy
- 1 | | yellow onion | produce
- 4 | clove | garlic | produce
- 2 | tbsp | fresh ginger | produce
- 2 | cup | basmati rice | dry
- 6 | | naan | bread
- 2 | tbsp | curry powder | dry
- 1 | tsp | paprika | dry
- 1 | tsp | coriander | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt and half the spices for 30 minutes.
2. Broil the chicken until charred in spots.
3. Cook onion, garlic and ginger, then the tomatoes and remaining spices for 15 minutes.
4. Add cream and chicken. Simmer 10 minutes and serve with rice and naan.
