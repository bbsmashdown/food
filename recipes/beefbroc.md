## Ingredients

- 1.5 | lb | flank steak | meat
- 2 | head | broccoli | produce
- 3 | clove | garlic | produce
- 1 | bunch | green onions | produce
- 2 | tbsp | fresh ginger | produce
- 2 | cup | jasmine rice | dry
- 2 | tbsp | brown sugar | dry
- 1 | tbsp | cornstarch | dry
- 1 | tbsp | sesame oil | dry
- 0.25 | cup | soy sauce | dry

## Steps

1. Slice the steak thin and toss with cornstarch.
2. Whisk soy sauce, brown sugar, ginger and garlic for the sauce.
3. Sear the beef in batches, then steam the broccoli in the pan.
4. Return everything with the sauce and cook 2 minutes. Serve over rice.
