## Ingredients

- 4 | | flour tortillas | bread
- 8 | oz | sliced turkey | meat
- 2 | | avocados | produce
- 2 | oz | baby spinach | produce
- 2 | tbsp | mayonnaise | dry

## Steps

1. Spread mayonnaise on the tortillas.
2. Layer turkey, avocado and spinach.
3. Roll tightly and slice in half.
