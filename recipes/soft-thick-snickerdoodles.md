## Ingredients

- 3 | cup | all-purpose flour | dry
- 2 | tsp | cream of tartar | dry
- 1 | tsp | baking soda | dry
- 1 1/2 | tsp | cinnamon | dry
- 1/2 | tsp | salt | dry
- 1 | cup | unsalted butter | dairy
- 1 1/3 | cup | granulated sugar | dry
- 1 | | egg | dairy
- 1 | | egg yolk | dairy
- 2 | tsp | vanilla extract | dry
- 1/3 | cup | granulated sugar | dry
- 1 | tsp | cinnamon | dry

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two large cookie sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.
2. Make the topping: stir together the 1/3 cup sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon in a small bowl.
3. Whisk the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon and salt together in a medium bowl.
4. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and 1 1/3 cup sugar on high speed for about 2 minutes, until smooth and creamy.
5. Add the egg, egg yolk and vanilla. Beat on medium-high until combined, scraping down the bowl as needed.
6. With the mixer on low, add the dry ingredients in 3 parts. The dough will be thick.
7. Roll the dough into balls of about 1 1/2 tablespoons (35 g) each. Roll each ball in the cinnamon sugar, and add extra on top if you like.
8. Place the balls 3 inches apart on the baking sheets.
9. Bake for 10 minutes. The cookies will be very puffy and soft. While still warm, press them lightly with the back of a spoon or fork to flatten.
10. Cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes, then move to a wire rack to cool completely.
11. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 7 days.
