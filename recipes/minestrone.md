## Ingredients

- 1 | | yellow onion | produce
- 3 | | carrots | produce
- 3 | stalk | celery | produce
- 2 | | zucchini | produce
- 4 | clove | garlic | produce
- 1 | bunch | kale | produce
- 1 | can | diced tomatoes | dry
- 2 | can | cannellini beans | dry
- 1 | cup | small pasta | dry
- 6 | cup | vegetable broth | dry
- 2 | oz | parmesan | dairy
- 2 | | bay leaves | dry
- 2 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Cook onion, carrot, celery and garlic in olive oil for 8 minutes.
2. Add tomatoes, broth and bay leaves. Simmer 15 minutes.
3. Add zucchini, beans and pasta. Cook 10 minutes.
4. Stir in kale and serve with parmesan.
