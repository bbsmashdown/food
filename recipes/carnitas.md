## Ingredients

- 3 | lb | pork shoulder | meat
- 2 | | oranges | produce
- 3 | | limes | produce
- 1 | | white onion | produce
- 5 | clove | garlic | produce
- 2 | | avocados | produce
- 2 | cup | jasmine rice | dry
- 1 | can | black beans | dry
- 2 | tsp | cumin | dry
- 1 | tsp | coriander | dry
- 2 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Rub the pork with spices and salt, and put it in the pot with onion, garlic and citrus juice.
2. Cook covered at 300°F for 3 hours, until it shreds easily.
3. Shred, then crisp the pork under the broiler for 5 minutes.
4. Serve over rice with beans and avocado.
