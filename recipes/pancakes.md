## Ingredients

- 2 | cup | flour | dry
- 2 | | eggs | dairy
- 1.5 | cup | milk | dairy
- 3 | tbsp | butter | dairy
- 2 | tsp | baking powder | dry
- 2 | tbsp | sugar | dry
- 0.5 | cup | maple syrup | dry

## Steps

1. Whisk the dry ingredients, then the wet, and combine without overmixing.
2. Cook ¼ cup scoops on a buttered griddle until bubbles form, then flip.
3. Serve with syrup.
