## Ingredients

- 1.5 | lb | chicken thighs | meat
- 1 | | white onion | produce
- 3 | clove | garlic | produce
- 1 | can | chipotles in adobo | dry
- 1 | can | crushed tomatoes | dry
- 12 | | corn tortillas | bread
- 2 | | limes | produce
- 1 | bunch | cilantro | produce
- 4 | oz | queso fresco | dairy
- 1 | tsp | cumin | dry
- 1 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Simmer the chicken in salted water for 20 minutes, then shred it.
2. Blend the tomatoes, chipotles, half the onion and the garlic with the cumin.
3. Sauté the remaining onion in olive oil, add the sauce and shredded chicken, and cook 10 minutes.
4. Warm the tortillas and serve with queso fresco, cilantro and lime.
