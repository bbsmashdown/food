## Ingredients

- 6 | | eggs | dairy
- 5 | oz | baby spinach | produce
- 1 | | bell peppers | produce
- 4 | | slices sourdough | bread
- 2 | tbsp | butter | dairy
- 0.5 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Soften the pepper in butter for 3 minutes.
2. Add spinach, then the beaten eggs. Stir gently until just set.
3. Serve on buttered toast.
