## Ingredients

- 1 | | baguette | bread
- 4 | tbsp | butter | dairy
- 3 | clove | garlic | produce
- 1 | bunch | parsley | produce
- 1 | oz | parmesan | dairy

## Steps

1. Mash butter with garlic, parsley and parmesan.
2. Spread on the split baguette.
3. Bake at 400°F for 10 minutes.
