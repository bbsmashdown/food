## Ingredients

- 8 | | eggs | dairy
- 1 | can | black beans | dry
- 8 | | corn tortillas | bread
- 1 | jar | salsa | dry
- 2 | | avocados | produce
- 1 | bunch | cilantro | produce
- 1 | cup | shredded Monterey Jack | dairy
- 1 | tbsp | olive oil | dry

## Steps

1. Warm the beans with a splash of salsa.
2. Crisp the tortillas in olive oil.
3. Fry the eggs. Stack tortillas, beans, eggs and salsa, then top with cheese, avocado and cilantro.
