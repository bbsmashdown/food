## Ingredients

- 2 | | ciabatta rolls | bread
- 8 | oz | mozzarella | dairy
- 2 | | tomatoes | produce
- 1 | bunch | basil | produce
- 1 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 0.5 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Split and toast the rolls.
2. Layer mozzarella, tomato and basil.
3. Drizzle with olive oil and season.
