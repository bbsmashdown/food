## Ingredients

- 2 | cup | jasmine rice | dry
- 3 | | eggs | dairy
- 12 | oz | frozen peas and carrots | dry
- 1 | bunch | green onions | produce
- 3 | clove | garlic | produce
- 1 | tbsp | sesame oil | dry
- 3 | tbsp | soy sauce | dry

## Steps

1. Use cold cooked rice if you have it.
2. Scramble the eggs and set aside.
3. Fry garlic and vegetables, add rice and soy sauce, then fold the eggs back in.
