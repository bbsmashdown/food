## Ingredients

- 2 | lb | chicken breasts | meat
- 1.5 | cup | breadcrumbs | dry
- 2 | | eggs | dairy
- 1 | jar | marinara | dry
- 8 | oz | mozzarella | dairy
- 2 | oz | parmesan | dairy
- 12 | oz | spaghetti | dry
- 1 | bunch | basil | produce
- 0.5 | cup | flour | dry
- 3 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry
- 0.5 | tsp | pepper | dry

## Steps

1. Pound the chicken to even thickness. Dredge in flour, egg, then breadcrumbs.
2. Pan-fry in olive oil until golden, about 4 minutes per side.
3. Top with marinara and cheeses. Bake at 425°F for 15 minutes.
4. Serve over spaghetti with basil.
