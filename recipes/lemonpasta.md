## Ingredients

- 12 | oz | linguine | dry
- 15 | oz | ricotta | dairy
- 2 | | lemons | produce
- 2 | oz | parmesan | dairy
- 5 | oz | baby spinach | produce
- 2 | clove | garlic | produce
- 2 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry
- 0.5 | tsp | pepper | dry

## Steps

1. Boil the linguine, saving a cup of pasta water.
2. Warm the garlic in olive oil, then wilt the spinach.
3. Stir ricotta, lemon zest and juice into the pan with a splash of pasta water.
4. Toss with the pasta and parmesan.
