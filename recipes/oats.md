## Ingredients

- 1 | cup | rolled oats | dry
- 1.5 | cup | milk | dairy
- 0.5 | cup | plain yogurt | dairy
- 1 | cup | blueberries | produce
- 2 | tbsp | honey | dry

## Steps

1. Stir oats, milk, yogurt and honey in a jar.
2. Chill overnight.
3. Top with blueberries in the morning.
