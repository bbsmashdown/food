## Ingredients

- 4 | | salmon fillets | meat
- 2 | head | broccoli | produce
- 1.5 | lb | baby potatoes | produce
- 1 | | lemon | produce
- 3 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1 | tsp | paprika | dry
- 1 | tsp | garlic powder | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry
- 0.5 | tsp | pepper | dry

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Halve the potatoes and roast them 15 minutes.
2. Add broccoli and salmon to the pan, seasoned with oil, paprika and garlic powder.
3. Roast 12 more minutes. Finish with lemon.
