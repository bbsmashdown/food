## Ingredients

- 1 | cup | navy beans | dry
- 1 | | onion | produce
- 1 | clove | garlic | produce
- 1 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1/2 | tsp | turmeric | dry
- 1/4 | tsp | hot red pepper | dry
- 1 | pinch | cinnamon | dry
- 1/2 | tsp | salt | dry
- 2 1/2 | tbsp | tomato paste | dry
- 1 | loaf | French bread | bread

## Steps

1. The night before, soak the navy beans in water overnight.
2. In the morning, saute the chopped onion and garlic in the olive oil.
3. Add the turmeric, hot red pepper, cinnamon and salt. Keep the cinnamon subtle.
4. Add the soaked beans, 2 to 3 cups of water and the tomato paste.
5. Simmer gently until the beans are done, checking the liquid and adding more water as needed.
6. Serve with toasted French bread for dipping in the sauce.
7. Optional: add a little meat cut in small bits to the sauce for extra flavor. The dish works fine without it.
