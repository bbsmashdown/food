## Ingredients

- 3 | | cucumbers | produce
- 2 | tbsp | rice vinegar | dry
- 1 | tbsp | sesame oil | dry
- 2 | clove | garlic | produce
- 1 | tbsp | soy sauce | dry
- 1 | tsp | sugar | dry

## Steps

1. Smash the cucumbers and break into pieces.
2. Toss with the vinegar, oil, garlic, soy sauce and sugar.
