## Ingredients

- 1.5 | lb | chicken breasts | meat
- 3 | tbsp | red curry paste | dry
- 2 | can | coconut milk | dry
- 2 | | red bell peppers | produce
- 8 | oz | snap peas | produce
- 2 | cup | jasmine rice | dry
- 1 | | lime | produce
- 1 | bunch | basil | produce
- 2 | tbsp | fish sauce | dry
- 1 | tbsp | sugar | dry

## Steps

1. Start the rice.
2. Fry the curry paste for a minute, then whisk in the coconut milk.
3. Add chicken and peppers. Simmer 12 minutes, add snap peas for the last 3.
4. Season with fish sauce, sugar and lime. Top with basil.
