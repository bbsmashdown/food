## Ingredients

- 1.5 | lb | shrimp | meat
- 3 | | bell peppers | produce
- 1 | | red onion | produce
- 8 | | flour tortillas | bread
- 2 | | limes | produce
- 0.5 | cup | sour cream | dairy
- 2 | tsp | chili powder | dry
- 1 | tsp | paprika | dry
- 1 | tsp | garlic powder | dry
- 2 | tbsp | olive oil | dry

## Steps

1. Toss the shrimp with spices and half the oil.
2. Sear peppers and onion in a hot pan for 6 minutes, then remove.
3. Cook the shrimp 2 minutes per side.
4. Serve in warm tortillas with lime and sour cream.
