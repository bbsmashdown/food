## Ingredients

- 2 | head | broccoli | produce
- 1 | oz | parmesan | dairy
- 2 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1 | tsp | garlic powder | dry
- 0.5 | tsp | salt | dry

## Steps

1. Toss florets with oil, garlic powder and salt.
2. Roast at 425°F for 20 minutes.
3. Finish with parmesan.
