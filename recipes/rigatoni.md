## Ingredients

- 1 | lb | rigatoni | dry
- 1 | lb | Italian sausage | meat
- 1 | can | crushed tomatoes | dry
- 1 | | yellow onion | produce
- 4 | clove | garlic | produce
- 0.5 | cup | heavy cream | dairy
- 4 | oz | parmesan | dairy
- 1 | bunch | basil | produce
- 2 | tbsp | olive oil | dry
- 1 | tsp | salt | dry
- 0.5 | tsp | pepper | dry

## Steps

1. Brown the sausage in olive oil, breaking it up.
2. Add onion and garlic, then the tomatoes. Simmer 15 minutes.
3. Boil the rigatoni until just tender.
4. Stir the cream into the sauce, toss with pasta, and finish with parmesan and basil.
