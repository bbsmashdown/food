## Ingredients

- 4 | | flour tortillas | bread
- 2 | cup | shredded Monterey Jack | dairy
- 1 | can | black beans | dry
- 1 | | red bell pepper | produce
- 1 | jar | salsa | dry

## Steps

1. Fill half of each tortilla with cheese, beans and pepper.
2. Fold and cook in a dry pan until golden on both sides.
3. Serve with salsa.
