Meal Planner update. Contents of this zip are the files that changed or are new.
Copy them over your cloned food folder (replace when asked), then commit and push.

NEW IN THIS UPDATE
- Recipe-only categories: dessert, baking, drink, other (filter buttons appear once you have one)
- Extras section on the Plan tab (any recipe, per week, adds to that week's grocery list)
- "Shop for this recipe" button on any recipe (one-off grocery list)
- Optional tags: line in recipes, tag buttons on the Recipes tab
- Soup and Barbecue buttons for the planner (settings.txt)
- Renamed to "Meal Planner" in the page title, breadcrumb, sidebar and home-screen name
- New recipe: On the Mend Spiced Red Lentil-Kale Soup (with photo)
- templates/recipe-template.md: a reference copy for writing recipes by hand (not published)
- Sides: a dropdown of all your sides under the dinner, with 1x/2x/3x and the full recipe shown; ingredients go on the grocery list
- Week view shows just the dish name, with the side as a subheading (no cuisine or servings)
- Optional household logins: one site, many families, each with a separate plan (set HOUSEHOLDS in Cloudflare; see README step 4). Nothing changes until you add it.
- Send to Reminders / Copy list / Share on the Groceries tab
- Settings: theme nights and the pantry list are now editable per household (defaults still come from settings.txt)
- README updated
- Settings moved to the bottom of the sidebar on desktop (phone keeps the link at the bottom of the page)
